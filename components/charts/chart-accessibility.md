## Accessibility (a11y)

> ℹ️ The design is WIP and will vary based on the platform and framework used.

Implementation may be challenging depending on the framework and can vary by platform.

> ℹ️ Our color palettes are fully accessible to color-blind people.

To achieve this, we based our design on several resources like the work of [Paul Tol](https://personal.sron.nl/~pault/) who created several color-blind palettes, or [ColorBrewer](https://colorbrewer2.org/#type=sequential&scheme=BuGn&n=3) who proposes palettes dedicated to maps.

Our palettes are tested using tools like [Viz Palette](https://www.susielu.com/data-viz/viz-palette).

### Color-blind friendly mode

[See how to activate this mode](https://zeroheight.com/626199550/p/502f2e-interactions/t/f58182030e)

To ensure our data visualization is accessible to every color-blind person, a color-blind mode can be activated.

As color can't be the only differentiation element, it's recommended to add a pattern on top of plain surfaces and use different lines dash, and/or dots to differentiate lines.

With this mode, every graph using a line will use a dashed line, and every graph displaying areas will add a pattern on top of the areas, allowing every color-blind person to differentiate the data set.

### Table format

[See how to display the table](https://zeroheight.com/626199550/p/502f2e-interactions/t/f58182030e)

To ensure everyone can understand your message, provide data in multiple formats like a table.

People learn differently - some visually, others through text. This accommodates various learning styles.
