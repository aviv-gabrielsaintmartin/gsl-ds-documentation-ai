Two blocks of a property listing detail page, mobile width. Build both, one after
the other on the same page. Bracketed names are dynamic data — keep them as they are.

## Block 1 — Energy and conditions

**Objective** — let a buyer judge, at a glance and before reading anything else,
how expensive the property will be to run and what condition it is in.

**Content**

- Section title: "Energy and conditions"
- Two ratings, one after the other. Each places this property on a 7-step scale
  running best to worst, with its own grade called out:
  - "[certificateType.DPE]" — this property is A
  - "[certificateType] GES" — this property is A
  - Some kind of data viz for the scale itself
- Six facts, each a label with its value or values:
  - Year of construction — [yearOfConstruction]
  - Last modernisation — [lastModernisation]
  - State of property — [ageState], [constructionStyle], [buildState]
  - Energy standard — [houseEnergyStandardType]
  - Heating method — [heatMethod] heating: [heatForm]
  - Energy sources — [energySource], [energyGeneration]
- These six are only part of what is known about the property. The buyer can ask
  for the rest — "See more".

## Block 2 — Finance

**Objective** — let a buyer work out whether they can afford this property and
what it would cost them each month, by adjusting a few numbers themselves, then
hand them over to a partner for a real quote.

**Content**

- Section title: "Finance"
- Three figures the buyer can change:
  - Property price — 560.000, in euros, pre-filled from the listing
  - Your contribution — 160.000, in euros, with the guidance
    "(20% of total costs recommended)"
  - Duration of our loan — 10 years, picked from a small set of durations
- The answer, and the most prominent thing in the block:
  "Estimated" · 2.256 € · "per month", carrying footnote marker 1
- What that monthly figure is made of — a data viz plus the three amounts, each
  one explainable on demand:
  - Amount borrowed — 000.000 €
  - Notary fees (8%) — 55.000 €
  - Interest cost (4,9%) — 0.000 €
- A partner offer, clearly set apart from the simulation above it:
  - "Find the right financing"
  - "Get a free, personalised mortgage estimate with our partner Credit Agricole"
  - Its action: "Customize simulation", carrying footnote marker 2

## Constraints

Mobile width. The amounts in the breakdown are placeholders in the source, not
real figures — keep them as given.
